🕵️ 1. Forensic Accounting & Investigation

📊 Net Worth Theory

  • Calculation: Gross Income = Increase in Net Worth + Personal Expenditure / Family Spending.
  • Adjustment: Cash hoarded from previous periods is subtracted from gross income.

💰 Expenditure Theory

  • Methodology: Compares total cash uses to total income to identify undisclosed / unreported funds.
  • Suitability: Most suitable when no books of accounts are maintained, records are inadequate, or the taxpayer is withholding records.
  • Exclusions: Ignores non‑cash expenses like depreciation and amortization.

🧠 Investigation using Intelligence

Focuses heavily on behavioral & background analysis – evaluating educational background, family pressures, lifestyle spending, companions.

🛡️ 3. Cyber Security & Digital Forensics

🎯 Threat Profiles & Attacks

ReconnaissanceUnauthorized discovery & mapping of systems, services, or vulnerabilities (e.g., port scans).
SpammerIndividual who sends large numbers of unsolicited bulk messages.
PhishingTrickery to gain sensitive information by spoofing a legitimate source.
VishingFraudulent phone call attempting to steal personal information (e.g., banking passwords/OTPs).
PhreakerManipulates the phone network to perform unauthorized functions.
Viruses vs. WormsWorms are self‑replicating & spread standalone; viruses require a host file.
ZombieCompromised computer/device within a botnet controlled remotely.
Denial of Service (DoS)Attack designed to saturate network links with spurious data, disabling services.
Cyber‑squattingRegistering a domain name intending to profit from someone else's trademark.
Port RedirectionAttack using a compromised host to pass traffic through a firewall that would otherwise be dropped.

🌐 Industry Cyber Landscape

  • Ethical Hacking Primary purpose is to identify system vulnerabilities proactively (White Hat).
  • Financial Sector Threats Recognized globally by G7, G20 Finance Ministers, and CPMI at BIS.
  • Trending Attacks Ransomware, supply chain attacks, phishing, and insider threats.
  • Mobile Threats A 2016 ITU/CGAP survey revealed 83% of Philippine, 56% of Ghanaian and 27% of Tanzanian mobile money users received fraudulent SMSs.
